■ 세차장 정보
주변 세차장 위치, 시설, 가격 정보를 확인하고 이용 후기를 남겨보세요. 다른 세차러들의 추천 세차장도 발견할 수 있어요.

■ 내차 관리
보유 차량을 등록하고 세차 기록을 체계적으로 관리하세요. 언제, 어디서, 어떻게 세차했는지 기록이 쌓이면 나만의 세차 히스토리가 완성됩니다.

■ 세차 루틴
나만의 세차 순서를 루틴으로 만들어 공유하세요. 단계별 세차 과정과 소요 시간을 한눈에 볼 수 있어요. 다른 사람들의 루틴을 참고해 나만의 완벽한 세차법을 완성해보세요.

■ 뱃지 시스템
활동할수록 다양한 뱃지를 획득할 수 있어요. 첫 피드 작성, 첫 리뷰, 10회 세차 등 세차 생활의 마일스톤을 뱃지로 기록하세요.

■ 세차지수

■ 팔로우 & 소통
마음에 드는 세차러를 팔로우하고 피드를 구독하세요. 팔로잉 전용 피드에서 관심 있는 사람들의 세차 기록만 모아볼 수 있어요.

### Promotional Text (170 chars)
A community for car wash enthusiasts! Share Before/After photos, review chemicals, discover car wash spots, build routines, and earn badges. Join the ultimate car wash community.

### Description (4000 chars)
Rain or shine, it's wash time! WashHub is a community app for people who love washing their cars.

■ Car Wash Feed
Share your car wash results with Before/After photos. Record which chemicals you used, where you washed, and connect with fellow enthusiasts. Support each other's work with likes and comments.

■ Chemical Info & Reviews
Find car wash product information in one place. Check honest reviews and ratings from real users to discover the perfect chemicals for your ride.

■ Car Wash Spots
Explore nearby car wash locations with facility details, pricing, and user reviews. Discover spots recommended by the community.

■ My Car Management
Register your vehicles and systematically manage your wash records. Track when, where, and how you washed to build your personal car wash history.

■ Wash Routines
Create and share your step-by-step wash routines. View each stage with estimated times. Learn from others' routines to perfect your own technique.

■ Badge System
Earn various badges as you participate! First post, first review, 10th wash — celebrate your car wash milestones with collectible badges.

■ Wash Index
Get real-time wash suitability scores based on weather data. We analyze rain forecasts, humidity, dust levels, and more to recommend the best time to wash.

■ Follow & Connect
Follow your favorite enthusiasts and subscribe to their feeds. Use the following-only feed to curate content from people you care about.

■ Privacy Protection
AI-powered face detection with selective mosaic when uploading images. Tap to select/deselect faces or drag to draw custom regions. All face detection runs on-device — nothing is sent to our servers.

Start your happier car wash journey with WashHub!
